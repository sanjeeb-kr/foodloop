How FoodLoop Works

A closed-loop system powered by atomic document operations and state-history tracking.

1

Donors Post Food

Canteens, bakeries, or banquets specify surplus servings, preparation time, and safe pickup deadlines with schema-validated documents.

2

Atomic Claiming

Verified NGOs claim available food via conditional single-document updates, avoiding double-claiming race conditions without table locks.

3

Pickup & Tracking

Receivers pick up surplus food and verify handover. Every transition is immutably logged into an embedded status history array.
